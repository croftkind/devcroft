Devcroft / Chat / proposed concept

A home for ideas, questions, and investigations.

Open Chat with the keyboard. Choose a space and harness. Organize the threads with tags.

Keyboard flow

Two familiar actions. One small dialog.

Organization

Several tags. The same conversation.

General keeps starting simple.

New chats begin in General. Adding a custom tag moves them into its group; removing every custom tag returns them to General.

Space and tags do different jobs.

Space owns the chat and chooses its account. Tags organize it. Changing tags or filters never changes the conversation’s owner.

Illustrative concept only. Buttons manipulate sample data in memory; no agent is launched, no folder is accessed, and refresh resets the demo. Rename/archive and full tag-management screens are specified in the plan, not simulated here.
